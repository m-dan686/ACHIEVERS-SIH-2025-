Community Awareness

💧 Cholera

Spread through contaminated water. Causes diarrhea and dehydration. Prevention: Boil water before drinking, wash hands regularly.

🤒 Typhoid

Caused by bacteria in unsafe water and food. Symptoms: fever, weakness, stomach pain. Prevention: Drink clean water, maintain hygiene, avoid street food.

🦠 Hepatitis A

Spread by consuming unsafe water and food. Symptoms: fever, jaundice, nausea. Prevention: Safe water, proper sanitation, vaccination.

🚱 Diarrhea

Common water-borne disease in children. Can cause dehydration. Prevention: ORS solution, safe drinking water, good hygiene.

✅ General Preventive Tips

  • Always boil or filter drinking water.
  • Wash hands before eating and after using toilets.
  • Store water in clean, covered containers.
  • Report suspected water contamination to local health workers.
